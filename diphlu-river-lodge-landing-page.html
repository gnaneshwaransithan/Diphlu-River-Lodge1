Diphlu River LodgeKaziranga National Park, Assam

Diphlu River Lodge sits directly on the boundary of Kaziranga National Park, on the banks of the Diphlu river in Assam — the only lodge built right at the park's edge, with no fence between its twelve stilted cottages and the forest beyond. Thatch-and-bamboo cottages raised on stilts, styled after Mishing tribal homes, open onto paddy fields and river views inside a UNESCO World Heritage landscape holding the world's largest population of the Indian one-horned rhinoceros.

02 — The Experience

Where the jungle begins at your veranda

Most safari lodges keep the forest at a safe distance beyond a fence line. Diphlu River Lodge doesn't. Built directly on the boundary of Kaziranga National Park, its twelve stilted cottages open onto the same unbroken corridor the rhino, elephant and tiger use — deer graze the paddy fields at dusk, hornbills call from the canopy at dawn, and the Diphlu river carries mist past every veranda.

📷 Dawn mist over the paddy field, cottage stilts in foreground — blue hour
📷 The Machan by lantern light — warm interior, bar & lounge

The design language borrows from the Mishing tribal villages of the Brahmaputra valley — bamboo and wood on raised stilts, thatched roofs, hand-woven textiles and lantern-lit walkways threading between a working rice paddy and the riverbank. It is jungle architecture built to disappear into its landscape, not dominate it.

Days follow the park's own clock: a pre-dawn jeep safari as mist lifts off the grasslands, a slow lunch on the Machan's river-facing verandah, an afternoon safari or a village walk, and dinner under a thatched roof with the Diphlu flowing past in the dark.

“Here, the fence was never built — so the jungle simply continues, uninterrupted, right up to the veranda step.” — The Ocean6 Field Journal

Two ways to wake by the river

Four of the lodge's twelve cottages face the Diphlu directly — thatched roofs on stilts, a folding glass door onto a private veranda with cane chairs, and the national park's tree line on the far bank. Air-conditioned and en-suite, with a writing desk, mini-bar and daily tea and coffee.

Paddy-View Cottages

The remaining eight cottages open onto the lodge's own working rice paddy, in the same stilted bamboo-and-wood style, with en-suite bath and private veranda. Four are semi-detached, suited to two travelling parties who want adjoining privacy.

For families, friends & first-time Kaziranga guests

Raised above the river, the Machan is the lodge's air-conditioned dining, bar and lounge pavilion, opening onto two verandahs — one over the water and forest, one over the farm for al fresco breakfasts. A library, a TV room and the day's jungle stories over dinner.

04 — The Kaziranga Advantage

India's rhino & tiger stronghold

Kaziranga is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and home to the world's largest population of the Indian one-horned rhinoceros, alongside one of the planet's highest densities of Royal Bengal tiger — roughly one per five square kilometres. Asiatic wild water buffalo, Eastern swamp deer, wild elephant and gaur share the same 884 sq km of grassland and forest.

The dolphin boat safari is Kaziranga's quieter counterpart to the jeep. A specially built country boat carries you onto the Brahmaputra in search of the endangered, playful Gangetic river dolphin — a couple of unhurried hours on open water, away from any engine dust or safari convoy.

Jeep safaris run in fixed morning and afternoon slots on routes set by the Forest Department, in open, windowless vehicles with an in-house naturalist aboard. Every safari at Diphlu is already built into the cost of your stay — this is where Ocean6 steps in, confirming your cottage, transfers and dates as one seamless itinerary.

Kaziranga's Rhino Capital

No park on earth holds more Indian one-horned rhinoceros than Kaziranga. Sightings on a jeep or boat safari come close to daily in season, though never guaranteed — grazing quietly in the grasslands the lodge itself opens onto.

09 — Questions, Answered

Frequently asked, plainly answered

Where is Diphlu River Lodge located?

On the banks of the Diphlu river, directly on the boundary of Kaziranga National Park in Assam, near Bagori Police Outpost — about 220 km (4 hours) from Guwahati, 2.5 hours from Jorhat and 1 hour from Tezpur.

What is the best time to visit Diphlu River Lodge?

November to April, when Kaziranga National Park is open to visitors. December to February brings the coolest weather and best birdwatching; the park closes for the monsoon roughly from May/June to October.

What kind of cottages does Diphlu River Lodge offer?

Twelve stilted, thatch-and-bamboo cottages built in the style of Mishing tribal homes — four river-facing, eight paddy-view (four of them semi-detached) — each air-conditioned, en-suite and sleeping two guests.

Are safaris included in the stay?

Yes. Jeep and dolphin boat safaris are included in the cost of your stay whenever Kaziranga National Park is open, accompanied by the lodge's in-house naturalists. Ocean6 reconfirms every safari slot before you travel.

What are the chances of seeing a rhino at Kaziranga?

Very good, though never guaranteed. Kaziranga holds the world's largest population of the Indian one-horned rhinoceros, alongside a high density of Royal Bengal tiger, wild elephant, gaur and swamp deer.

How do I reach Diphlu River Lodge from Guwahati?

By road via NH37, roughly 220 km and about 4 hours. Ocean6 arranges private transfers from Guwahati, Jorhat or Tezpur airports, timed to your cottage's readiness.

Is Diphlu River Lodge good for honeymoons and birdwatchers?

Yes. Couples favour the unfenced riverside setting and candlelit Machan dinners; birders come for the over 400 species recorded across the park and roughly 57 seen from the lodge grounds itself. Ocean6 tailors the experience mix to your occasion.
